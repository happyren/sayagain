# Governance

## Current model

Say Again is maintained by a single maintainer (listed below). Decisions are
made in the open through issues, discussions and architecture decision
records in `docs/adr/`. Anyone may propose an ADR; the maintainers accept or
reject it with a written rationale.

| Role       | GitHub    |
| ---------- | --------- |
| Maintainer | @happyren |

## Adding maintainers

A contributor who has landed several substantive changes and reviewed
others' work over at least three months may be nominated. Existing
maintainers decide by consensus.

## Specification

`spec/intent-metadata.md` is versioned independently of the code. The intent
is for the convention to outlive this implementation: the project will
propose it to the Model Context Protocol community as a specification
enhancement, and will not object to the namespace being superseded by a
protocol-native mechanism.

## Neutral home

If the project reaches the point where a vendor-neutral home would help
adoption, the maintainers intend to donate it to a foundation (the Agentic AI
Foundation under the Linux Foundation is the natural candidate). The DCO,
Apache-2.0 license and trademark policy are chosen to make that transfer
possible without relicensing.
